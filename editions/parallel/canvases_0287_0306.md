[![Canvas 287](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0287.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0287.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 249 (numbered '249' at top right and bottom center; headers 'Epileptia' / 'ffallyng Sicknes'): Galenic treatise on the three kinds of epilepsy (Epilepsia, Analeptia, Cataleptia), distinguishing the greater and lesser forms, prognostic signs (including age/menstruation in women), and humoral diagnostic signs (phlegm, choler, and melancholy).*
