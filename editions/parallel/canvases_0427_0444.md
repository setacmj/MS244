## Canvas 428 — Unnumbered (Pedigree)

[![Canvas 428](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0428.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0428.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Genealogical pedigree of the Talbot family (Earls of Shrewsbury and Talbots of Grafton, dated 1601 at the foot), drawn in pen-and-ink roundels linked by marriage and descent lines (including a hatched/crenellated descent line for the Grafton/Albrighton branch). Includes 5 blank roundels in the upper-middle area and a 7-line historical note in the centre detailing the marriage and coheirs of Edmund Beaufort, Duke of Somerset, and Eleanor, daughter of Richard Beauchamp, Earl of Warwick. No original page number is visible.*

## Canvas 429 — Unnumbered (Armorial B)

[![Canvas 429](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0429.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0429.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Armorial grid (5 columns by 6 rows, 30 cells) for families beginning with 'B' (Bray to Batayll). Nine cells contain painted/emblazoned coats of arms in black and faded red/pink (Bracy, Bekeringe, Bamnue, Burgoyne, Banke, Bourgilam, Belsted, Blaknham, Borgat, Barkesworth — 10 painted cells in total) and one cell (Bemclere) contains a faint pencil/ink sketch of a beast. Top-right and bottom-right corners are torn and repaired with conservation paper.*
