## Canvas 187 — 149

[![Canvas 187](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0187.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0187.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 149 (numbered '149' at top right and 'Cxlix' at bottom center; running header 'Palsey'): Two columns on paralysis (palsy). Left column contains a Latin medical treatise distinguishing paralysis from apoplexy and spasm, followed by Cura, Nota, Causa palisis, Vnguentum ad palisim, Ad Paralisin Lingue, Optime vng[uentu]m ad palisim, and a note on provoking vomiting. Right column contains Electuarum ad palisim, Oleum de ligno fraxini, Medicina mirabilis in palisim, Ad paralisin, 'ffor lymbes Benumed and lame' (with ☾ ☾ marks), and 'ffor the Palsy. F. ffolliott' (dated 1628).*
