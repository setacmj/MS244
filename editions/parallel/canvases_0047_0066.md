## Canvas 47 — p. 9

[![Canvas 47](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0047.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0047.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 9 (numbered '9' at top right): Devoted to Scabies ('Scabies'). Left column gives Latin humoral diagnostics (blood, choler, salt phlegm) and bloodletting rules (cephalic vs. hepatic vein), followed by mercurial and lead ointments. Right column gives black ointment ('vnguent[um] nigrum') with mercury sublimate, a quicksilver woollen-girdle remedy with fumitory whey, and Unguentum Saracenicum.*
