[![Canvas 27](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0027.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0027.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Preliminary leaf 11r (numbered '11' at upper right): Continues Latin chemical/medical preparations. Left column: calcination of sapphire ('Sapphirum cum duplo pondere sulphuris'), 'Oleum Sapphiri' (citing 'pa. 299. r. 2.'), and 'De Amianthe Lapide'. Right column: 'De Crystallo' (virtues of rock crystal for dysentery, nursing mothers, and gravel) and 'Ad calculum' (distilled/calcined crystal with sugar of roses).*
