## Canvas 67 — 29

[![Canvas 67](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0067.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0067.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 29: 'Brestes' / 'Breastes' (cross-referenced to p. 103). Left column details a red-lead and oil black plaster for sore breasts, rolled on a wet board with a slick-stone, and notes on syringing broken breasts with myrrh and white wine. Right column gives an internal styptic of powdered white dog dung in beer/ale to stop running breasts, a per-rosin and deer-suet tenting salve, oil of mallows (attributing it to 'Eliz S. ... my grandmother'), a fenugreek and linseed cataplasm, cleansing washes (Aegyptiacum / Apostolorum), and two pen-and-ink diagrams at the foot labelled 'y^e tent made' and 'the tente vn made'.*
