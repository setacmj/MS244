## Canvas 367 — 339

[![Canvas 367](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0367.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0367.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 339 (numbered '339' at top right): Plague and Pestilence receipts ('Plague' / 'Pestilens'). Left column includes malmsey-based plague drinks, a preservative and curative regimen also good for smallpox, measles, and sweating sickness, Pilulae Pestilentiales (aloes, myrrh, saffron), an experiment with herb perdon, an 'Exelent medecyne p[ro]ved in the desease in hervest 1571' (none that took it died), and a Latin receipt with purslane and rose waters. Right column includes treacle with zedoary and tormentil (citing p. 333), a Tewksbury family receipt ('proued by my grandfather & my ffather & my selfe in Tewksbury'), terra sigillata and dragon's blood given at the assizes, a receipt from 'Eliz[abeth] Nicolas my grandmother', and 'A Souerayne medecyne' baked in an eggshell with saffron, mustard seed, nux vomica, dittany, tormentil, and camphor.*

## Canvas 368 — 340

[![Canvas 368](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0368.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0368.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 340 (numbered '340' at top left and bottom center): Plague and Pestilence receipts ('Pestelens' / 'Plague'). Left column contains a receipt using boiled rooks in verjuice, a receipt revealed by God to King Henry V ('Henry .5. Rex Anglie') using betony, marigolds, mallow, and treacle, a detailed autobiographical account of the plague in 'Esham' (Evesham) in the house of John Malstross where the compiler advised discharging a musket morning and evening in the chamber (citing a citadel discharging ordnance), and a receipt from Alexis of Piedmont ('out of Alexis') using dried ivy berries. Right column contains roasted onion with treacle, an aloetic preservative, 'Aqua Imperialis p[ro] pesti', juniper berries and Lemnian earth, making an issue in the arm or leg, Hiera Picra Galeni, and leaves of yew/rue in lemon juice with angelica root.*
