## Canvas 87 — 49

[![Canvas 87](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0087.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0087.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 49 (also numbered 'xlix' at bottom center; no 'Waters' running header): Left column has 'A water for heate & pymples', 'water off Coloworte for sores', 'A water to be vsed w[i]t[h] the Empl. de minio pa. 51', 'Mr Baskerwile his water' (referencing p. 61), 'Aqua Diuina ad ulc. glu. bon.' (attributed to Fernelius), and 'Alliter ex Srofeldo' (referencing p. 47). Right column has 'A graye Water' (amalgam of aqua fortis and quicksilver), 'A water to make fleshe growe in a Soore', 'Aqua miratina pulueribus' (balm distilled with white wine), clinical notes referencing p. 46 and proposing frog-spawn water, and 'A clensing water' (two Latin recipes).*

## Canvas 103 — 65

[![Canvas 103](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0103.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0103.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 65 (Recto), headed 'Bleedinge off Greene woundes to Stoppe:' and numbered '65' at top right (also '.lxv.' at bottom centre): Two-column treatise and receipt collection on stopping bleeding in fresh ('green') wounds. Left column sets out the six surgical principles of wound healing (stopping blood, dry stitching, conglutinating without tenting/probing, incarnating, cicatrizing), followed by styptics using hot burnt-coal dust, bole Armeniac with egg white and flax, oak/eaves excrescences, spider cobwebs dipped in Sanguis Veneris, and heron down. Right column gives Latin and English styptic powders (including Lanfranc's), linen wet in frog spawn (highlighted by a manicule in the right margin), Philonium Persicum for nosebleeds, Lanfranc's caustic powder, burnt linen in boiled wine, dried human blood, and copperas/sandiver.*
