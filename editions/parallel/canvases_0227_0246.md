*Page 204 (numbered '204' at top left and 'cciiij' at bottom centre): Dedicated to remedies for the Black Jaundice ('Jaundys / Black'). Includes herbal decoctions, celandine/soap/gander-dung drink with a live March cock applied to the back, Lady Salop's earthworm powder, a baked malmsey pot used by 'kyng C.' (King Charles?), a live tench applied to the back and ribs, lesser daisy, baked snails, alum, honey/nettle/saffron, and earthworm oatmeal porridge.*

[![Canvas 245](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0245.JP2/full/600,/0/default.jpg)](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19608652_MS_244_0245.JP2/full/1400,/0/default.jpg)

*Page 207 (numbered '207' at top right and 'ccvij' at bottom centre): Left column continues Yellow Jaundice remedies ('yealowe Jaundies'), including turmeric and old hard cheese, a Latin horehound decoction ('Experimentum contra ictericiam'), fumitory and scabious syrup, a distilled herbal water with spermaceti, bryony root in white wine, a cross-reference to 'pa. 182' for a dried toad laid on the liver, green goose and chicken dung powders, and two Latin prescriptions ('Potio purgans ad Icteritiam' and 'Pil. ad idem') by Dr. John Sadler. Right column is blank beneath the running header.*
